2. Write a short policy summary (roughly 300 to 500 words) as a constant in app/triage.py or a separate prompts module, with each policy stated in one line and tagged with its source article name.
3. Read the existing system prompt in app/triage.py that drafts the reply, and add the policy summary to it, plus an instruction to answer only from the summary, to cite the article name, and to say "I'm not sure, a teammate will follow up" when the summary does not cover the question.
4. Keep the classification call (billing / bug / how-to) separate from the draft call so the new policy text only affects the reply and does not change classification behavior.

8. Run a small set of sample tickets (about 10 to 15 across the three categories, including some the docs do not cover) against the real model to confirm replies match the articles and out-of-scope tickets are handled with the fallback, then adjust the summary wording as needed.
