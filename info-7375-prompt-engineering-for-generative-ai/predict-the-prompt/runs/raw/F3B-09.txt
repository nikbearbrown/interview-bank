5. Keep the classification output structured (a JSON-shaped response or a tool schema) and parse it, so the label and the reply draft stay separate. Leave the `save_reply(ticket_id, text)` call and the `POST /triage` contract in `app/api.py` unchanged.

8. Run the test suite, then try a handful of real tickets (one per category plus one that no article covers) and compare the replies against the articles by hand. Check token usage and cache-hit numbers in the response to confirm the caching works before shipping the container.
